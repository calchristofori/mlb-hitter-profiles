# Wilyer Abreu — RF, Boston Red Sox · Age 27 (2026) · Bats L / Throws L

## Top line
Abreu is a lift-and-barrel power hitter who gets his value from extra-base damage rather than batting average. His contact skills and swing rates are average, but he launches the ball well enough to rank near the top fifth of the league in expected power. In 2026 he moved from a platoon role to everyday at-bats and held his level.

## Expected stats
His xwOBA (house model), an estimate of what his contact, strikeouts and walks should have produced, was .354 in 2026. That ranked in the 73rd percentile and was his best full season, up each year since .330 in 2024. Power drives it. His xBA (house model) of .252 sits right at the qualified average, while his xISO, the extra bases his contact should produce per at-bat, ranked 25th of 135. His wOBA has trailed his xwOBA for two straight seasons, so his results likely understate the skill.

## Batted-ball profile
His damage comes from launch, not top-end exit velocity. His EV90 of 105.3 is only slightly above average, but his LA median of 21.0 degrees is far above the qualified 14.7. His launch spread is a bit tighter than average, which turns his contact into barrels at an 83rd-percentile rate. He is not a pull slugger. His Pull% is below average, and his Pulled Air%, the share of batted balls lifted to the pull side, is exactly average. The trend needs watching. His EV90 fell from 107.5 in 2025, his Pulled Air% from 25.7%, and his Hard-hit% has declined two straight years. Rising Sweet spot% and more opposite-field contact suggest this is partly a deliberate all-fields adjustment, and his Barrel% held steady.

## Plate discipline
His swing profile is league average. He swings at strikes, chases and misses at almost exactly qualified rates, so his IZ Swing − Chase (in-zone swing rate minus chase rate) is average as well. The real change is contact. His K% has fallen every year, from 28.0% in 2024 to 20.7%. His in-zone swing rate dropped back from its 2025 high, and his walk rate is modestly above average.

## Platoon profile
His role changed in 2026. After facing lefties well below the rate of other left-handed hitters in 2024 and 2025, his share jumped to 31.9%, above the 25.1% average for left-handed hitters. He held up, with a .359 xwOBA against lefties and .352 against righties. He is a different hitter against each side. Against lefties he makes more contact with less power (16.6% K%, 103.0 EV90), and against righties he does more damage and strikes out more. He reads as an everyday bat.

## Vs. pitch type
Breaking balls are his best pitch group. His .363 xwOBA against them ranks in the 92nd percentile, backed by a better-than-average whiff rate and a 14.2% Barrel%. Pitchers appear to know it, throwing him breaking balls well below the league share. They feed him fastballs instead, at 61.2% against a 55.2% average and rising slightly each year. That is where he is vulnerable. His fastball Whiff% of 23.7% is in the bottom quarter of qualified hitters, and his fastball results run slightly below average.

## Synthesis
Abreu wins by putting the ball in the air at productive angles. He does not have elite exit velocity or a pull-heavy approach. His average swing rates and improved contact put enough balls in play for that launch to produce barrels, which keeps his xSLG high while his xBA sits at league average. His defining strength is that efficiency, especially against breaking balls, which he punishes. His biggest vulnerability is missing fastballs, and pitchers are already leaning on the pitch.

The 2026 season adds two positives. He cut his strikeouts again, and he handled the left-handed pitching he had previously been kept away from. The concern is that his EV90, Hard-hit% and Pulled Air% all slipped together. Some of that likely reflects heavier exposure to lefties, against whom his exit velocity is lower, and a shift toward all-fields contact. Still, his EV90 against righties was also below his 2025 level. At 27 this is not an aging signal, but if his exit velocity keeps falling, his power output depends even more on launch efficiency.

## Sample notes
He had 447 PA in 2024, 417 in 2025 and 681 in 2026. His 2026 splits were 217 PA against lefties and 464 against righties. The 2026 pitch-group results rest on 375, 166 and 136 PA ending on fastballs, breaking balls and offspeed pitches. Data runs through 2026-09-27.
