# Wilyer Abreu — RF, Boston Red Sox · Age 27 (2026) · Bats L / Throws L

## Bottom line
Abreu is a fly-ball, barrel-first hitter. His value comes from lifting the ball at a productive angle more than from top-tier raw power. His 12.1% Barrel% ranks 24th of 135 qualified hitters, and his xwOBA (house model) of .354 sits in the 73rd percentile. He hits spin well. His hole is the fastball: his whiff rate against it is in the bottom quarter of qualified hitters, and pitchers throw him more fastballs than the league average.

## Who he is
He lives in the air. His LA median of 21.0 degrees sits far above the qualified average of 14.7. His FB% (35.8% vs 27.8%) and GB% (33.2% vs 41.0%) confirm a hitter who rarely beats the ball into the ground.

His EV90 of 105.3 is only modestly above average (57th percentile), so the damage likely comes from angle more than force:
- **Sweet spot%:** 36.3%, 70th percentile.
- **LA SD:** 27.1, tighter than average, which points to a consistent swing path.
- **Barrel%:** well above average.

That shape shows up in his expected stats (house model), which estimate production from exit velocity and launch angle. His xSLG of .476 ranks 31st, while his xBA of .252 is a hair below average. He is a power-over-average bat.

His approach is roughly league average. His IZ Swing − Chase is .381 against a .374 average; the metric subtracts chase rate from in-zone swing rate, and higher is better. His BB% is a modestly above-average 10.4%, and his K% has fallen from 28.0% in 2024 to 20.7%.

His 2026 batted balls changed shape. Pulled Air%, the share of batted balls hit in the air to the pull side, fell to exactly league average at 19.4%. His Pull% dropped and his Oppo% rose. At the same time, his LD% climbed to 22.4% from 18.1%. This reads as a shift toward all-fields, line-drive contact at the expense of pull-side lift.

## How pitchers beat him
Fastballs are the answer. Against them:
- **Whiff%:** 23.7%, well above the 18.8% average and 108th of 135.
- **xwOBA:** .358, below the .368 average.
- **Chase%:** 28.1%, roughly average, but up each year from 18.2% in 2024.

That suggests he is increasingly swinging through fastballs and offering at more of them off the plate. Pitchers are already leaning in: his fastball share of 61.2% is above the 55.2% qualified average and has edged up each season.

Spin is the wrong approach against him. His breaking-ball xwOBA of .363 ranks 12th of 135, with a 14.2% Barrel%. His offspeed EV90 of 106.2 sits in the 84th percentile.

Bringing in a lefty is no answer either. He posted a .359 xwOBA over 217 PA against LHP and .352 against RHP, with a lower K% against lefties. He is an everyday bat, not a platoon piece.

## What to watch
In 2026, his EV90 fell from 107.5 to 105.3 and his Pulled Air% from 25.7% to 19.4%. Two markers of a slowing bat moved together, and his Hard-hit% has also declined two straight years. At 27, with his Whiff% flat and Barrel% holding, this reads more like an approach change than decline. Still, whether his top-end exit velocity holds matters most for a hitter whose value rests on barrels, especially with his fastball whiffs already a target.
