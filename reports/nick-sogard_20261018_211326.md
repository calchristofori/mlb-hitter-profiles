# Nick Sogard — 1B, Boston Red Sox, Age 28, Bats S / Throws R

## Bottom line

Sogard is a contact-and-discipline hitter. He rarely whiffs, rarely chases and walks at an above-average rate, but he does little damage when he connects. He lifts and pulls the ball more than most hitters, though his exit velocity sits well below the qualified average. His biggest vulnerability is breaking balls, which he takes in the zone and struggles to punish.

## Profile

**Sogard is a pull-and-lift hitter without much power.** In 2026 (277 PA, a reliable read), his EV90 (90th-percentile exit velocity, a gauge of top-end damage) was 100.7 against a qualified average of 104.6. His Hard-hit% was 28.6% against 41.0%, and his Barrel% was 3.7% against 8.3%.

The shape of his contact is better than the force behind it:
- **Pulled Air%** (share of batted balls hit in the air to the pull side) was 23.8%, above the 19.4% average.
- **Pull%** was 50.8%, against 44.1%.
- **Sweet spot%** was 37.6%, against 34.6%.

His approach is patient to the point of passivity. He chases little and makes contact often, but he also lets a lot of strikes go by. He has 1127 pitches of data in 2026. He was not qualified, so no league ranks or percentiles are available, and every comparison here is to the qualified average.

## Skill breakdown

**Contact quality: below average.**
- His xwOBA of .318 trails the .338 qualified average.
- His xBA (house model) of .253 sits right at the .254 average, so he finds hits at a normal rate.
- His xSLG (house model) of .364 is well under the .430 average. The shortfall is extra-base damage, not contact.
- His Avg EV of 86.8 trails the 89.4 average.

**Batted-ball shape: elevated, with a popup leak.**
- His LA median of 16.0 sits above the 14.7 average, and his LA SD of 27.0 is slightly tighter than the 27.9 average.
- His FB% of 22.8% is below the 27.8% average, and his GB% of 39.2% is near the 41.0% average.
- His PU% jumped to 10.1% from 2.8% in 2025.
- Interpretation: he lifts the ball to the pull side, but without the exit velocity to carry it. Some of that lift turns into popups rather than fly balls.
- His sprint speed of 27.6 is roughly average (27.4 qualified average), so his legs add little to his value.

**Swing decisions: selective, but passive in the zone.**
- His Chase% of 23.6% is well below the 29.4% average, and his BB% of 11.2% beats the 9.5% average.
- His Whiff% of 20.4% is better than the 23.9% average, and his K% of 20.2% is near the 20.7% average.
- The catch is his IZ Swing% of 56.5%, far under the 66.8% average.
- That passivity drags his IZ Swing − Chase (in-zone swing rate minus chase rate; higher is better) to .329, below the .374 average.
- Interpretation: he gives away hittable strikes. With limited power, those taken pitches may be where some of his value goes missing.

## Performance by pitch type

**Fastballs: contact without damage (157 PA ending, reliable).**
- He rarely misses: Whiff% of 15.6% against an 18.8% average, and Chase% of 21.1% against 27.8%.
- His contact is soft for the pitch: EV90 of 101.9 against 105.1, Hard-hit% of 30.6% against 45.1%, and Barrel% of 2.8% against 8.9%.
- His xwOBA of .345 trails the .368 average. His wOBA of .382 is well ahead of it (+.037), so his fastball results have outrun his contact quality.

**Breaking balls: the soft spot (66 PA ending).**
- His xwOBA of .253 sits far under the .303 average, and his K% of 30.3% is worse than the 26.8% average.
- His Whiff% of 25.0% is actually better than the 30.2% average.
- The issue is passivity: his IZ Swing% against breaking balls is 48.0%, against a 64.7% average.
- Interpretation: he takes breaking strikes, falls into deep counts and strikes out looking or defending, rather than swinging through them.

**Offspeed: his best relative group (54 PA ending, a thin but valid sample).**
- His xwOBA of .319 beats the .299 average, and his Whiff% of 27.6% is better than the 29.9% average.
- Pitchers still throw him offspeed at a 20.4% share, above the 14.4% average, matching his 21.0% share in 2025.
- They throw him fewer breaking balls (27.1% vs. 30.5%) and fewer fastballs (52.5% vs. 55.2%) than average.

**Platoon:** the vs. LHP sample (73 PA) is too small to read. Against righties (204 PA) his xwOBA was .316, close to his overall mark.

## Strengths and concerns

**Strengths**
- Contact skill: Whiff% of 20.4% vs. a 23.9% average, and 15.6% on fastballs.
- Zone control: Chase% of 23.6% and BB% of 11.2%, both better than average.
- Useful shape: Pulled Air% of 23.8% and Sweet spot% of 37.6%, both above average.

**Concerns**
- Limited power: EV90 of 100.7 vs. 104.6, and Barrel% of 3.7% vs. 8.3%.
- Passivity: IZ Swing% of 56.5% vs. 66.8%.
- Breaking balls: xwOBA of .253 and K% of 30.3% against them.

## Outlook

His EV90, Pulled Air% and xwOBA all edged up across three seasons, and his Whiff% has held steady, so there is no sign of a slowing bat at 28. The one thing to watch is his in-zone swing rate. It has fallen each season, and whether he starts attacking more hittable strikes will shape how much his contact skill turns into damage.
