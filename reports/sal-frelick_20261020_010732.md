# Sal Frelick — RF, Milwaukee Brewers · Age 26 (2026) · Bats L / Throws R

## Top line
Frelick is a contact-first, ground-ball hitter who almost never misses but rarely hits the ball hard enough, or high enough, to do damage. His value rides on putting the ball in play and on above-average speed, and his output sagged in 2026 when the lift he found in 2025 went away.

## Expected stats
His xwOBA (house model; what his contact, strikeouts and walks should produce) of .283 sits well below the .338 qualified average, down from a career-best .298 in 2025. He gets there through contact rather than power. His xBA (house model) of .238 is a bit under average. His xISO, the extra bases his contact should produce per at-bat, is .090 against a .175 average, which marks him as a singles hitter. His 2025 results ran well ahead of expected (+.034), so much of this year's wOBA drop looks like that gap closing rather than a lost skill.

## Batted-ball profile
This is low-exit-velocity, low-launch contact with an inconsistent path. His EV90 of 101.1 trails the 104.6 average, though it has edged up across his career. His median launch angle of 7.5 degrees is roughly half the league norm, and he hits about half his batted balls on the ground. His LA SD is wider than average, which suggests the balls he does elevate are about as likely to be popped up as driven. Pulled Air% (balls in the air to the pull side) fell to 14.4% from 19.0%, and Sweet spot% fell with it, giving back the 2025 gain that had put him near the league norm. His 28.8 ft/s sprint speed is above average and likely helps a grounder-heavy profile.

## Plate discipline
Frelick swings less than average both in and out of the zone, and he makes contact at an elite rate when he does. His 59.3% IZ Swing% is well under the 66.8% average. That zone passivity, not chasing, keeps his IZ Swing − Chase (in-zone swing rate minus chase rate) below average. His 10.1% Whiff% is under half the league rate, and his K% has fallen each season to 9.9%. Walks remain a bit below average.

## Platoon profile
At 98 PA, his 2026 line against left-handers is too small for a read. Against right-handers, his split is essentially his full-season profile: a .276 xwOBA with the same low launch and modest exit velocity. The packet cannot settle whether he needs a platoon partner.

## Vs. pitch type
He has no swing-and-miss hole. His whiff rates are far below average against fastballs, breaking balls and offspeed. The weakness is damage against fastballs. His .292 xwOBA on them trails the .368 average, and his Hard-hit% on fastballs is well short of the norm despite a 9.3% whiff rate. Pitchers appear to have noticed. Fastballs made up 66.0% of what he saw, above the 55.2% average and up from 59.7% in each of the prior two seasons. He also takes zone fastballs more often than average. His offspeed results are too small a sample to read.

## Synthesis
Frelick's game is a chain of contact without impact. He is selective, takes plenty of strikes and rarely misses, so he puts a high share of plate appearances in play. That contact comes off the bat at below-average exit velocity, mostly on the ground, with a launch spread wide enough to waste some of the air balls. The result is an xBA that hovers near average and an xISO among the thinnest you'll see. His bat-to-ball skill is real and has strengthened every season. His biggest vulnerability is that pitchers can attack him in the zone with fastballs, because the contact he makes on them rarely does damage.

The trend reads as a launch regression, not a slowing bat. His EV90 rose and his Whiff% fell in 2026, both healthy signs at age 26. Only Pulled Air% moved the wrong way. His 2025 peak lined up with more pulled lift, which suggests his ceiling depends on recovering that launch rather than on making more contact.
