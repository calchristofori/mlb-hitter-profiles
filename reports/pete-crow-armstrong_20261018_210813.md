# Pete Crow-Armstrong — CF, Chicago Cubs, Age 24 (2026), Bats L / Throws L

## Bottom line

Crow-Armstrong is a pull-side, air-ball power hitter. He led all 135 qualified hitters in Pulled Air% in 2026 and pairs that with above-average force, which produced an xwOBA (house model) of .383, in the 91st percentile. His vulnerability is swing-and-miss and chasing. His strikeout rate sits in the 20th percentile, and pitchers can get fastballs past him and get him to expand the zone against breaking balls.

## Profile

He wins by lifting the ball to his pull side. He does it often, and he does it hard enough to matter.

- **Ball flight:** His LA median of 22.0° is far above the qualified average of 14.7°.
- **Batted-ball mix:** His GB% (31.3% vs. 41.0%) is well below average, and his FB% (33.6% vs. 27.8%) is well above it.
- **Direction:** His Pull% of 54.9% compares with a 44.1% average.
- **Power:** His EV90 (106.6, 75th percentile) is above average but not elite. The damage comes as much from where he hits the ball as from top-end force.
- **Speed:** He ranks 6th in sprint speed at 29.5 ft/s.

His approach changed sharply in 2026:
- IZ Swing% fell to 69.1% (down 12.1 pts).
- Chase% fell to 33.1% (down 8.6 pts).
- BB% rose to 11.4% (up 6.9 pts).

## Underlying vs. results

His 2026 wOBA (.406, 2nd of 135) ran ahead of his xwOBA (.383, 13th), a gap of +.023. A BABIP jump of +.051 to .327 helped open that gap. He is a very good hitter whose results were a step above his contact quality. The gap was widest against lefties, at +.053.

## Skill breakdown

**Contact quality.** His expected stats point to power more than batting average:
- **xSLG (house model): .516**, 94th percentile.
- **xBA (house model): .264**, 65th percentile.
- **Barrel%: 12.9%**, 90th percentile.
- **Hard-hit%: 48.8%**, 85th percentile.
- **Sweet spot%: 38.3%**, 87th percentile.

Hard-hit% has risen 5.1 and 6.9 pts in consecutive seasons. The platoon split is real:
- **vs. RHP (496 PA):** xwOBA .406, EV90 107.3.
- **vs. LHP (230 PA):** xwOBA .334, EV90 105.0.

**Batted-ball shape.** Pulled Air% counts fly balls, line drives and popups hit to the pull side, as a share of his batted balls. His 34.8% ranks 1st of 135; the qualified average is 19.3%. That is the core of his game.

His LA SD of 27.1 is slightly tighter than average (66th percentile), so his high launch is fairly consistent rather than spread between grounders and sky-high flies. The cost of that launch shows in popups: PU% has climbed from 6.0% to 9.5% to 11.7%. He also uses the opposite field less, with Oppo% falling to 18.3%.

**Swing decisions.** IZ Swing − Chase is his in-zone swing rate minus his chase rate, so higher is better. His .360 sits below the .374 average, in the 37th percentile.

- **Chase%:** 33.1%, 28th percentile.
- **Whiff%:** 26.3%, 35th percentile. Below average, but improving.
- **K%:** 25.8%, 20th percentile.

His new selectivity cut zone swings more than it cut chases, so the decision score slipped even as walks rose. He still swings in the zone more than average (69.1% vs. 66.8%).

## Performance by pitch type

All three pitch groups have usable samples in 2026. Against each one, his damage on contact is good to elite; his losses come from whiffs and chases.

**Fastballs (378 PA ending, 53.6% share).**
- His xwOBA of .375 is only slightly above the .368 average (61st percentile). His wOBA of .419 ran well ahead of it.
- His fastball Whiff% of 23.0% against an 18.8% average puts him in the bottom quarter (23rd percentile), and his K% on fastballs is in the 14th percentile.
- He does not chase fastballs: his Chase% of 24.8% is in the 67th percentile.
- The problem is missing hittable fastballs, not expanding on them.

**Breaking balls (214 PA ending, 30.0% share).**
- When he connects, he crushes them: Hard-hit% 52.5% (96th percentile), Barrel% 16.1% (93rd) and xwOBA .360 (90th).
- His Chase% of 40.4% ranks 123rd of 135 (9th percentile), and his K% on breaking balls is 35.5% (15th percentile).
- Breaking balls left in the zone get punished; breaking balls off the plate are how pitchers put him away.

**Offspeed (126 PA ending, 16.4% share).**
- This is his best group. His xwOBA of .439 ranks 1st of 135, with an EV90 of 106.8.
- He chases these too: his Chase% of 40.2% is in the 18th percentile.
- He makes enough contact to avoid damage from the chasing (Whiff% 60th percentile).

**How pitchers attack him.** His pitch mix is close to league average for every group. Breaking-ball share rose to 33.6% in 2025, then eased to 30.0%, so pitchers have not settled on a sustained adjustment.

## Strengths and concerns

**Strengths**
- He has the league's best Pulled Air% at 34.8%.
- His power is elite on secondary pitches: offspeed xwOBA .439 (1st) and breaking Hard-hit% 52.5% (96th percentile).
- His contact quality keeps rising: EV90 went 101.9 → 105.2 → 106.6.

**Concerns**
- He misses fastballs: fastball Whiff% 23.0%, 23rd percentile.
- He chases spin: breaking-ball Chase% 40.4%, 9th percentile, which feeds a K% of 25.8%.
- Results outran contact: wOBA .406 vs. xwOBA .383, with a .334 xwOBA against lefties.

## Outlook

The trend is upward. EV90, Pulled Air% and Whiff% are all moving in the right direction, with no decline signals at age 24. The thing to watch is chasing breaking balls. His damage on contact is already near the top of the league, so fewer chases would directly lower a strikeout rate that is his main drag.
