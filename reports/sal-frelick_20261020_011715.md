# Sal Frelick — RF, Milwaukee Brewers · Age 26 (2026) · Bats L / Throws R

## Top line
Frelick is a contact-first, ground-ball hitter whose value comes almost entirely from putting the ball in play: he rarely misses, rarely strikes out and rarely chases. What he does not do is drive the ball, and with modest exit velocity spent mostly on the ground, his expected output sits well below average.

## Expected stats
His xwOBA (house model; expected wOBA from contact quality, strikeouts and walks) of .283 is well below the .338 qualified average and down from a career-best .298 in 2025. His xBA (house model) is only slightly below average despite his tiny strikeout rate, because weak contact gives back much of what his contact rate earns. The real gap is power: his xISO (expected extra bases per at-bat) is .090 against a .175 average. His .332 wOBA in 2025 ran well ahead of expectation, while his 2026 results ran slightly behind it. His expected stats moved far less, so they are the better read on his skill.

## Batted-ball profile
This is a low-impact, ground-ball profile. His EV90 (top-end exit velocity) of 101.1 trails the 104.6 qualified average, and his Hard-hit% and Barrel% sit far below average. He launches the ball low, with a median launch angle well under the league's and half his batted balls on the ground. His launch spread is also wider than average, so even his better contact is often topped or popped up. His overall direction is roughly league-average. His Pulled Air% (pulled fly balls, liners and popups) fell to 14.4% from 19.0% in 2025, alongside a lower launch angle and fewer balls in the sweet spot. EV90 has crept up every season, so this reads as lost lift rather than a slowing bat.

## Plate discipline
His discipline is built on contact more than selectivity. His Whiff% of 10.1% compares with a 23.9% qualified average, and it has fallen every season. He chases less than average, but he also swings at strikes far less often (59.3% against 66.8%). As a result, his IZ Swing − Chase (in-zone swing rate minus chase rate) lands slightly below average, which suggests passivity as much as discipline. The payoff is a 9.9% K%, but his walk rate still trails the average.

## Platoon profile
Milwaukee has used him as an everyday player. His share of PA against lefties ran below other left-handed hitters in 2024, then rose above theirs in 2025 and matched it in 2026. His 98 PA against lefties this season are too few for a read. Against righties he is the hitter described above, with a .276 xwOBA, and by usage he is an everyday bat.

## Vs. pitch type
Fastballs expose the limits of his profile. He almost never misses them, but his fastball xwOBA (.292) and Hard-hit% are well below the qualified average, and he takes more in-zone heaters than most hitters. Pitchers appear to have noticed: fastballs rose to 66.0% of what he saw, up from 59.7% in each of the prior two seasons and well above the 55.2% average. Breaking balls follow the same pattern of excellent contact with below-average damage. Offspeed results are too thin to read, though he rarely misses those pitches either.

## Synthesis
Frelick wins by putting the ball in play. Elite-level bat-to-ball skill and a low chase rate keep strikeouts rare, and his above-average sprint speed helps a profile that hits half its balls on the ground. But low exit velocity, low launch and an inconsistent launch angle mean that contact rarely becomes extra bases. That caps his xISO and leaves his value riding on batting average, which explains why his results swing with BABIP.

His biggest vulnerability is a lack of damage, and it shows most against fastballs. Pitchers are challenging him in the zone more often, betting he will make contact without hurting them, and his passive in-zone approach does little to discourage it. His 2025 season showed his best version: more pulled balls in the air produced his strongest expected output, and that lift receded this year. With EV90 rising and whiffs falling at age 26, nothing here suggests a slowing bat. Whether he climbs back toward average production appears to hinge on his launch profile.
