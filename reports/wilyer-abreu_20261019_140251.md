# Wilyer Abreu — RF, Boston Red Sox · Age 27 (2026) · Bats L / Throws L

## Bottom line
Abreu is a fly-ball damage hitter. His contact rate is average, but he lifts the ball consistently, so good-but-not-elite exit velocity turns into barrels and above-average expected power. That shape produced a .354 xwOBA (house model) in 2026, up from .347. His hole is the fastball: he misses it far more than most qualified hitters, and pitchers throw it to him more often than average.

## Who he is
He wins on damage, not contact. His xBA (house model) of .252 sits just under the .254 qualified average. His xISO, the extra bases his contact should produce per at-bat, is .224, 25th of 135.

Launch drives that power more than raw force does. His EV90 (90th-percentile exit velocity) of 105.3 is only modestly above the 104.6 average. But his LA median of 21.0 is far above the 14.7 average, and his GB% of 33.2% is well below the 41.0% average. His LA SD (27.1, 66th percentile) is also tighter than average, which means he repeats that lift. The payoff is a 12.1% Barrel%, 24th of 135.

In 2026 he also used more of the field. Pulled Air%, the share of batted balls hit in the air to his pull side, fell to exactly the league-average 19.4%. His Pull% dropped below average and his Oppo% rose.

His approach sits in the middle of the pack. IZ Swing − Chase (in-zone swing rate minus chase rate) was .381 against a .374 average. His K% matched the average at 20.7%, and his BB% was a bit better at 10.4%. The real progress is in contact: his K% has fallen from 28.0% in 2024 to 20.7%.

## How pitchers beat him
Fastballs are the hole. His fastball Whiff% of 23.7% ranks 108th of 135, in the bottom quarter, against an 18.8% qualified average. His fastball K% of 19.5% ranks 92nd.

In 2026 he swung less often at fastballs in the zone (65.6%, down from 73.8%) and chased them more often (28.1%, up from 18.2% in 2024 and 22.9% in 2025). Pitchers are leaning in. Fastballs made up 61.2% of what he saw, against a 55.2% average, edging up from 59.8% and 60.9%. The damage hasn't disappeared. His fastball xwOBA of .358 is only slightly under the .368 average, and his fastball Barrel% of 11.5% is above average. That suggests a contact problem more than a power one.

Breaking balls are the opposite. He posted a .363 xwOBA against them (12th of 135) and a 14.2% Barrel% (15th), yet pitchers throw them to him only 24.2% of the time, well below the 30.5% average.

Platoon side doesn't define him: he posted a .359 xwOBA against lefties and .352 against righties. Against lefties he trades power for contact (xISO .195, K% 16.6%). Against righties it's the reverse (xISO .237, EV90 106.0). He reads as an everyday bat.

## What to watch
Watch his exit velocity. His EV90 fell 2.2 mph to 105.3, his Hard-hit% has slid from 51.1% to 43.5% over three seasons, and his Pulled Air% dropped 6.3 pts. On the other hand, his Whiff% held and his Sweet spot% rose 4.4 pts as more balls went the other way. At 27, that looks more like an all-fields adjustment than a slowing bat. Still, his power rests on lift rather than force, so EV90 is the number to track.
