# Steven Kwan — CF, Cleveland Guardians · Age 28 (2026) · Bats L / Throws L

## Top line
Kwan is an extreme contact-and-control hitter: he almost never chases or misses, and he repeats a line-drive launch better than anyone, but he hits the ball with the lowest exit velocity of any qualified hitter. His value comes from batting average and, increasingly, walks, with almost no power behind it.

## Expected stats
His xwOBA (house model, expected wOBA from contact quality, strikeouts and walks) of .332 sits just under the qualified average of .338. He gets there almost entirely through contact. His xBA (house model) ranks 19th of 135, while his xISO, the extra bases his contact should produce per at-bat, is .067, second-lowest among qualified hitters. The xwOBA climbed from .305 even as xISO fell to a career low. That suggests the gain came from walks, not better contact. His wOBA tracks his xwOBA closely, so his 2026 results look like an honest read of his skills.

## Batted-ball profile
Kwan has the most repeatable launch in the league, paired with the softest contact. His LA SD (launch angle spread) and Sweet spot% both rank first of 135. He sprays line drives to all fields at a near-average launch angle, with Pulled Air% (pulled balls in the air) in the bottom fifth. But his EV90 of 95.3 ranks last, and his Hard-hit% of 10.4% compares with a 41.0% qualified average. The trend is the concern: EV90 fell from 98.2 in 2025, Pulled Air% dropped, and Hard-hit% and Barrel% fell sharply. What little damage he used to produce has largely disappeared.

## Plate discipline
He is one of the league's best at staying in the zone and making contact, and in 2026 he became far more selective. His Chase% and Whiff% both rank second of 135. His IZ Swing% fell to 51.5% from 58.8%, well below the 66.8% qualified average. That leaves his IZ Swing − Chase (in-zone swing rate minus chase rate) in the 16th percentile, a sign that he takes strikes rather than chases. The result is a 9.7% K% and a walk rate that jumped to 13.3%.

## Platoon profile
Cleveland plays him every day. His share of PA against lefties, 32.2%, is well above the average for left-handed hitters and has stayed that way for three seasons. Against lefties he whiffs more often (11.3% vs 6.8%) and his xwOBA dips to .320 from .337 against righties, but his contact shape is the same. He is an everyday bat with a modest platoon drop-off, not a platoon candidate.

## Vs. pitch type
Pitchers are challenging him with fastballs. Their share rose to 67.9%, well above the 55.2% average. He holds his own against them, with an xwOBA near the qualified average, while swinging at fewer than half of the fastballs he sees in the zone. Breaking balls and offspeed pitches produce weaker results, and his offspeed xwOBA has been below average three straight seasons. Neither group falls into the bottom quarter, though, and his whiff rates rank near the top of the league on all three groups. Pitchers have no single pitch to exploit. Instead, his EV90 ranks last against every group, so they can throw strikes without fear of damage.

## Synthesis
Kwan wins by refusing to give away plate appearances. He rarely chases, rarely misses, and lands the ball in a productive launch window more consistently than anyone. That combination lifts his xBA near the top of the league despite the weakest exit velocity on record among qualified hitters. With almost no extra-base damage, his value rests on singles and on-base skill.

In 2026 that balance shifted. As his contact softened, he stopped swinging at strikes, and pitchers responded by pouring in fastballs. The extra walks more than offset the lost contact quality, so his overall output rose. Still, a profile built on walks and soft line drives has little margin. The two warning signs, a falling EV90 and a falling Pulled Air%, moved together this season. At 28, age is not the obvious explanation, and his stable whiff rate suggests his bat-to-ball skill is intact. The drop in exit velocity, though, reads more like lost bat speed than a chosen approach. That is the trend to watch.
