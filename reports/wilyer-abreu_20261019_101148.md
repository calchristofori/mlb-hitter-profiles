# Wilyer Abreu — RF, Boston Red Sox · Age 27 (2026) · Bats L / Throws L

## Bottom line
Abreu drives value through lift and barrels. He is a fly-ball hitter whose 12.1% Barrel% and .476 xSLG rank in the top quarter of qualified hitters, even though his top-end exit velocity is only modestly above average. His best skill is damage against breaking balls. His main vulnerability is swing-and-miss on fastballs, the pitch he sees most, and 2026 also brought a dip in top-end exit velocity and pulled lift.

## Profile
Abreu is an above-average bat whose value runs through slugging. In 2026 (681 PA) his xwOBA (house model; expected wOBA based on exit velocity and launch angle) was .354, against a qualified average of .338 (73rd percentile). The split is lopsided. His xSLG (house model) of .476 ranks 31st of 135, while his xBA (house model) of .252 sits just below average. He is a damage hitter, not a contact hitter.

The four foundational metrics show how he gets there:
- **EV90: 105.3.** This is only slightly above average (57th percentile), so his raw ceiling is good but not elite.
- **Contact shape: elite.** His LA median of 21.0 is far above the 14.7 average, and his LA SD of 27.1 is tighter than average (66th percentile). He consistently gets the ball into the air at useful angles.
- **Pulled Air%: 19.4%.** This measures air balls hit to the pull side, where most damage happens. His mark landed exactly at league average in 2026, down from 25.7% in 2025.
- **IZ Swing − Chase: .381.** This is a swing-decision score: in-zone swing rate minus chase rate. His mark is roughly average (54th percentile).

**Interpretation:** he is a lift-first, all-fields fly-ball hitter. His barrels come from efficient launch more than raw force. In 2026 his approach spread out. Pull% dropped to 41.0%, below the 44.1% average, and Oppo% rose to 30.5%.

## Underlying vs. results
His results trailed his contact quality for a second straight year. His 2026 wOBA of .340 sat below his .354 xwOBA (−.014), following a −.010 gap in 2025, with a BABIP of .285. Park factors are not in the database, so Fenway's effect on his fly balls cannot be checked.

## Skill breakdown
**Contact quality.** His contact converts to damage better than his exit velocities alone suggest:
- Barrel% of 12.1% (83rd percentile) and Sweet spot% of 36.3% (70th) both beat average comfortably.
- Hard-hit% of 43.5% is only modestly above average.
- The hard-contact trend is negative. Hard-hit% fell from 51.1% in 2024 to 44.9% to 43.5%, and Avg EV fell from 91.6 to 90.8 to 89.8.

**Batted-ball profile.** He lives in the air:
- FB% is 35.8% against a 27.8% average, and GB% is 33.2% against 41.0%.
- PU% of 8.6% is the cost of that lift, though it is down from 10.5% in 2025.
- In 2026 he traded some fly balls for line drives (LD% 22.4%, up from 18.1%), and Sweet spot% rose 4.4 pts.

**Speed.** At 28.0 ft/s (68th percentile), his legs add a little value beyond the bat.

**Plate discipline.** His approach is average across the board:
- Chase% of 29.5% and Whiff% of 24.6% both sit near league norms.
- His IZ Swing% fell 6.0 pts to 67.6%, which drove the drop in his swing-decision score. That makes him slightly more passive in the zone, not more disciplined.
- His results improved anyway. K% fell for a second straight year, to a league-average 20.7%, and BB% of 10.4% is above average.

## Performance by pitch type
**Breaking balls are his strength.** Across 166 PA ending on breaking pitches, he ranked near the top of the league:
- xwOBA of .363 ranks 12th of 135 (92nd percentile).
- Barrel% of 14.2% sits in the 90th percentile.
- Whiff% of 25.1% beats the 30.2% average.

This is new. His breaking-ball Whiff% sat above 31% in both 2024 and 2025. Pitchers already throw him breaking balls less than average (24.2% share vs. 30.5%).

**Fastballs are the pressure point.** Across 375 PA ending on fastballs, his production was serviceable but he missed too often:
- xwOBA of .358 is slightly below average (44th percentile), with an above-average Barrel% of 11.5%.
- Whiff% of 23.7% ranks in the bottom quarter of qualified hitters (20th percentile), and his Chase% on fastballs is average.
- Pitchers lean on the pitch. His fastball share of 61.2% is above the 55.2% average and has risen slightly each season.

**Offspeed: good contact, poor results.** Across 136 PA ending on offspeed pitches:
- EV90 of 106.2 and Hard-hit% of 44.8% both rank in the 83rd–84th percentile.
- His xwOBA of .327 is above average, but his wOBA of .281 lags it.
- He is aggressive here: an 85.4% IZ Swing% and an above-average 35.1% Chase%.

## Strengths and concerns
**Strengths**
- Lift and barrels: 12.1% Barrel% and .476 xSLG, both top-quarter.
- Breaking-ball damage: .363 xwOBA, 92nd percentile.
- Steadily lower strikeout rate: K% down to 20.7%.

**Concerns**
- Fastball whiffs: 23.7%, bottom quarter, on the pitch he sees 61.2% of the time.
- Fading hard contact: Hard-hit% down to 43.5%, Avg EV to 89.8.
- Pulled Air% fell to 19.4%, from 25.7%.

## Outlook
His overall level is rising, but two bat-speed markers fell together in 2026: EV90 dropped 2.2 mph and Pulled Air% dropped 6.3 pts, while Whiff% was essentially flat. At 27, this reads at least as plausibly as a deliberate shift to an all-fields approach as a slowing bat, given his higher Sweet spot% and Oppo%. The one thing to watch is whether his EV90 rebounds toward 107; if it doesn't, his slugging will rest heavily on launch efficiency.
