# Wilyer Abreu — RF, Boston Red Sox · Age 27 (2026) · Bats L / Throws L

## Bottom line
Abreu is a fly-ball damage hitter who wins through efficient launch rather than raw force. His Barrel% ranks in the 83rd percentile of qualified hitters, while his EV90 sits near average. He hits breaking balls as well as almost anyone, but he misses fastballs at a bottom-quarter rate, and pitchers already throw him more fastballs than average.

## Who he is
Abreu's value is power. His xwOBA (house model, which estimates value from contact quality, walks and strikeouts) of .354 ranked 37 of 135 qualified hitters. That value comes from his xSLG (house model) of .476 (78th percentile), not his xBA (house model) of .252, which sits just below the .254 average. He lives in the air. His LA median of 21.0 is far above the 14.7 average, and his GB% of 33.2% is well under the 41.0% norm. His launch is also tighter than most, with an LA SD in the 66th percentile.

The engine is angle, not force. His EV90 of 105.3 is only slightly above the 104.6 average, yet his Barrel% of 12.1% far exceeds the 8.3% average. That rate has held across three seasons at 10.5%, 12.0% and 12.1%, so it reads as a real skill rather than a one-year spike. He does not rely on pulling the ball. His Pulled Air% (balls in the air to the pull side, where most damage happens) is exactly average at 19.4%, and his Pull% runs below average. His approach is middle of the pack. His IZ Swing − Chase (in-zone swing rate minus chase rate) of .381 is near the .374 average, and his K% of 20.7% matches the average.

## How pitchers beat him
Fastballs are the hole. His fastball Whiff% of 23.7% ranks 108 of 135 (20th percentile), against an 18.8% average. His xwOBA on fastballs of .358 also trails the .368 average across 375 PA ending on the pitch, so the swing data and the results agree. His fastball Chase% has climbed from 18.2% to 22.9% to 28.1%, and pitchers have noticed. Fastballs made up 61.2% of what he saw, above the 55.2% average and up each season.

He punishes spin. His xwOBA on breaking balls of .363 ranks 12 of 135 against a .303 average, with a Barrel% of 14.2% (90th percentile). Pitchers throw him breaking balls at a 24.2% share, well under the 30.5% norm. He is not a platoon case. He posted a .359 xwOBA against lefties in 217 PA and .352 against righties in 464 PA, so he is an everyday bat.

## What to watch
EV90 and Pulled Air% fell together in 2026. Hard-hit% has also slid two straight seasons, from 51.1% to 44.9% to 43.5%. At 27, this reads more like an adjustment than decline: his Sweet spot% rose 4.4 pts and his Oppo% climbed, which fits a more all-fields approach. Because barrels carry his profile, the key question is whether his Barrel% holds with less top-end force behind it.
