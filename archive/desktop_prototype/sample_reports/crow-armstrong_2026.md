# Pete Crow-Armstrong — CF, Chicago Cubs
Age 24 (2026 season) · Bats L / Throws L

## Bottom line
Crow-Armstrong is an above-average hitter right now, and the improvement is backed by underlying skill, not luck. His xwOBA (house model) rose from .285 to .335 to .383 over three seasons, ranking 13th of 135 qualified hitters in 2026. The engine is power to the pull side: he led all qualified hitters in pulled fly balls (34.8%) while pushing EV90 to 106.6 mph. The watch item is his swing-decision score, which is below average even after a big drop in chases.

## Profile and role
Pull-side air-ball power hitter with elite speed. EV90 of 106.6 mph is in the "good" band (league qualified average 104.6). His median launch angle of 22° puts most contact in the air, and 34.8% of his batted balls are pulled in the air, against a qualified average of 19.4%. His sprint speed of 29.5 ft/s is near elite (average is about 27). In 2026 he walked much more than before (11.4% BB, up from 4.5%), which supports a top-of-the-order or middle-of-the-order role.

## Skill breakdown
- **Contact quality:** xwOBA .383, xSLG .516, xBA .264. EV90 106.6 mph (34th of 135), hard-hit rate 48.8%, barrels 12.9% of batted balls. All have improved for three straight years.
- **Batted-ball shape:** fly balls 33.6%, ground balls 31.3%, line drives 23.4%, popups 11.7%. Sweet-spot rate (8–32°) rose to 39.7% from 34.0% in 2024. LA SD is 27.1, slightly tighter than the qualified average of 27.9, so the swing is consistent.
- **Swing decisions:** chase rate fell sharply, from 41.8% to 34.2%, but in-zone swing rate fell even more, from 81.3% to 68.7%. The net score (.345) is below the qualified average (.368) and ranks 93rd of 135. Whiff rate improved to 26.3%, which is in the "solid" band.

## Strengths and concerns
**Strengths**
- Pull-side air power: 34.8% pulled fly balls, first among 135 qualified hitters.
- Rising damage ceiling: EV90 up from 101.9 to 105.2 to 106.6 mph.
- Better plate discipline: chase rate down 7.6 points, walk rate up from 4.5% to 11.4%.

**Concerns**
- Passive in the zone: in-zone swing rate of 68.7% (down 12.6 points) drags his decision score below average (.345 vs .368).
- Popups: 11.7% of batted balls, up from 6.0% in 2024. That's the cost of an air-ball approach.
- Possible BABIP regression: .327 in 2026 against .276–.292 in 2024–25, a one-year jump.

## Outlook
Every skill trend points up except in-zone aggression. At 24 there are no aging or decline signals: EV90 is rising, whiff rate is falling and pulled fly-ball rate is rising. **The one thing to watch:** whether he can get back to swinging at strikes (around 75%+ in-zone swing rate) without giving back the chase gains. If he does, his decision score moves to above average and the power plays up further.
